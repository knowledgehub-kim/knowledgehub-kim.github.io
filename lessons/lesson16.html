본문 읽기 · Reading

The Past and the Now

Adler believed that our past does not decide our future. We may be influenced by our childhood, our failures, or painful experiences, but they do not have to control the rest of our lives. Instead of asking, “Why am I like this?”, Adler encourages us to ask, “What can I do from now on?” We cannot change the past, but we can change what we do with it.

The Courage to Choose

Knowing that the past does not decide your future is not enough. You also need the courage to choose how you will live today. Sometimes we stay in an unhappy situation because we are afraid of change. We may say, “This is just who I am,” or “It is too late for me.” But Adler believed that we can choose a new direction. A small choice today can become the beginning of a different life.

Choose Your Feelings

Adler also believed that our feelings are connected to our choices and purposes. Imagine that someone says something rude to you. You may feel angry, but you can still choose how to respond. You can shout back, stay calm, or walk away. We cannot always control what happens to us, but we can choose our attitude and response. Taking responsibility for that choice gives us more freedom in our lives.

토론 질문 · Discussion Questions

  1. Do you think your past determines your future? Why or why not?

    과거가 미래를 결정한다고 생각하나요? 왜 그렇게 생각하나요?

  2. Have you ever made a small choice that changed your life?

    작은 선택이 당신의 삶을 바꾼 경험이 있나요?

  3. When someone is rude to you, how do you usually respond?

    누군가 당신에게 무례하게 대할 때, 보통 어떻게 반응하나요?

  4. If you could choose a new direction in your life, what would you change?

    삶에서 새로운 방향을 선택할 수 있다면, 무엇을 바꾸고 싶나요?

예시 답변 · Sample Answers

  1. No, I don't think my past determines my future. My past has influenced me, but I can still make different choices today.

    아니요, 과거가 미래를 결정한다고 생각하지 않습니다. 과거가 나에게 영향을 주기는 했지만, 오늘 나는 여전히 다른 선택을 할 수 있습니다.

  2. Yes. I once decided to try something I had been afraid of. It was a small decision, but it gave me more confidence.

    네. 한때 두려워했던 일에 도전하기로 결심한 적이 있습니다. 작은 결정이었지만, 그 선택은 저에게 더 큰 자신감을 주었습니다.

  3. I used to get angry easily, but now I try to stay calm. I cannot control other people, but I can choose my response.

    예전에는 쉽게 화를 냈지만, 지금은 침착하려고 노력합니다. 다른 사람을 통제할 수는 없지만, 나의 반응은 선택할 수 있습니다.

  4. I would like to try new things without worrying too much about failure. I want to be more open to change and enjoy new experiences.

    실패를 너무 걱정하지 않고 새로운 것에 도전하고 싶습니다. 변화에 좀 더 열린 마음을 가지고 새로운 경험을 즐기고 싶습니다.
