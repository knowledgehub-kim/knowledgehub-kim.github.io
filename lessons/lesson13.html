회화 패턴 · Speaking Patterns

1. You never know where ~ may lead you.

~이 어디로 이어질지는 아무도 모릅니다.

You never know where a new experience may lead you.

새로운 경험이 어디로 이어질지는 아무도 모릅니다.

2. What seems ~ today may become ~ someday.

오늘 ~처럼 보이는 것이 언젠가는 ~이 될 수 있습니다.

What seems useless today may become meaningful someday.

오늘 쓸모없어 보이는 것이 언젠가는 의미 있는 것이 될 수 있습니다.

본문 읽기 · Reading

Steve Jobs dropped out of college, but he didn't stop learning. He took a calligraphy class because he thought it was beautiful. At that time, he didn't know why he was learning it. He simply followed his interest and intuition. It was a small “dot” in his life that seemed useless at the time.

Years later, when Jobs and his team were developing the first Macintosh computer, he remembered what he had learned in that calligraphy class. They added beautiful fonts and carefully designed typography to the Macintosh. If Jobs hadn't taken that class, the Macintosh might not have had such beautiful typography. A small dot from his past had connected to something important in his future.

You cannot connect the dots looking forward. You can only connect them looking backward. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future. Have the courage to follow your heart and intuition, even when you are not sure where they will lead you. Something you are doing today may become meaningful someday.

토론 질문 · Discussion Questions

  1. What was a “useless dot” in your past that became helpful later?

    과거에는 쓸모없어 보였지만 나중에 도움이 되었던 ‘점’은 무엇이었나요?

  2. Steve Jobs followed his intuition. Do you usually trust your intuition or logical facts? Why?

    스티브 잡스는 자신의 직관을 따랐습니다. 여러분은 주로 직관을 믿나요, 아니면 논리적인 사실을 믿나요? 그 이유는 무엇인가요?

  3. If you could go back to your 20s, what new “dot” - a skill, hobby, or experience - would you add to your life?

    20대로 돌아갈 수 있다면, 어떤 새로운 ‘점’ - 기술, 취미 또는 경험 - 을 삶에 더하고 싶나요?

  4. What “dot” are you creating in your life today?

    여러분은 오늘 자신의 삶에 어떤 ‘점’을 만들고 있나요?

예시 답변 · Sample Answers

  1. Learning to use computers seemed unimportant at first, but it later helped me a lot at work.

    처음에는 컴퓨터를 배우는 것이 중요하지 않아 보였지만, 나중에 직장에서 큰 도움이 되었습니다.

  2. I usually look at the facts first, but I also listen to my intuition when I have to make a difficult decision.

    저는 보통 사실을 먼저 살펴보지만, 어려운 결정을 내려야 할 때는 제 직관에도 귀를 기울입니다.

  3. I would learn another language and travel more. I think those experiences would help me see life from different perspectives.

    다른 언어를 배우고 여행을 더 많이 하고 싶습니다. 그런 경험들이 삶을 다양한 관점에서 바라보는 데 도움이 될 것 같습니다.

  4. Learning English is one of the dots I am creating today. I don't know exactly where it will lead me, but I believe it will open new possibilities.

    영어를 배우는 것은 제가 오늘 만들고 있는 점들 중 하나입니다. 이것이 정확히 어디로 이어질지는 모르지만, 새로운 가능성을 열어 줄 것이라고 믿습니다.

Every experience can become a part of your story.

모든 경험은 당신의 이야기의 한 부분이 될 수 있습니다.
