A. Vocabulary Review

Choose the best word from the box.

2. Money and __________ are not the only measures of success.

4. Starting something new often takes __________ and patience.

1. Viktor Frankl - Finding Meaning in Life

What did Frankl believe we can still choose when we cannot change a difficult situation?

a. Our past b. Our attitude c. Other people d. Our circumstances

a. Having more money than others b. Becoming famous

c. Having balance, meaningful work, relationships, and hope d. Working as much as possible

a. They had more soldiers than the enemy. b. They won without losing a single soldier.

a. How quickly you succeed b. Whether you keep moving c. How old you are d. What other people

think

c. Because success can lose meaning if we lose health, relationships, or peace of mind.

d. Because we should stop working hard.

a. Through success and money b. Through small choices and habits

c. Through our jobs d. Through other people's opinions
