B. Reading & Ideas - Choose the best answer.

1. According to Adler, which statement is closest to his idea?

a. Our past completely determines our future.

b. We can choose our response and the direction of our lives.

c. We cannot change our attitude.

2. What lesson does Lincoln's humor teach us?

a. Humor makes every problem disappear.

b. Humor can change the way we face problems and weaknesses.

c. Humor is useful only in political debates.

3. What does 'connecting the dots' suggest?

a. Past experiences may become meaningful later.

b. Every experience must be useful immediately.

c. We should avoid uncertain experiences.

4. Which idea is shared by the lessons about exercise, nature, and hardship?

a. Small actions and experiences can gradually change us.

b. Our future is completely outside our control.

c. Change happens only through major events.
